Biography

About

Download CV (PDF)

Dr. Nitesh Kumar is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics, Applied Science Cluster, School of Engineering at UPES, Dehradun. He is an Associate of the Inter-University Centre for Astronomy and Astrophysics (IUCAA), coordinates the IUCAA Centre for Astronomy Research and Development (ICARD) at UPES, and is a Life Member of the Astronomical Society of India.

He received his Ph.D. in Astronomy and Astrophysics from the University of Delhi in 2025, supervised by Prof. H. P. Singh and Prof. Philippe Prugniel (University of Lyon). His research applies computational and machine-learning methods to stellar photometric and spectroscopic data, using model-based interpolation and full-spectrum analysis to study RR Lyrae variables and characterise stars in globular clusters.

At UPES he teaches astrophysics, computational physics and programming, builds simulation-based teaching labs, and organises astronomy schools and workshops with IUCAA.
