Research

Stars that pulse, and the clusters they live in

Model-based interpolation and full-spectrum analysis, applied to pulsating stars and the clusters that host them. Pick a theme to see its papers.

More about my research
01

RR Lyrae variability

Light-curve modelling, photometric metallicities and stellar-parameter estimation for RR Lyrae stars.

RR Lyrae / light curves / metallicity Papers on this theme 02

Machine learning & model interpolation

Neural networks that interpolate grids of pulsation models and spectra, recovering physical parameters directly from observations.

ANN / TensorFlow / scikit-learn Papers on this theme 03

Globular clusters & stellar spectroscopy

Full-spectrum fitting and multiband photometry of the stellar populations of M3, M53 and NGC 6397.

M3 / M53 / NGC 6397 Papers on this theme 04

Observations & surveys

Co-PI of time-series photometry and spectroscopy of RR Lyrae stars in M3 with the 3.6 m Devasthal Optical Telescope (two cycles), alongside Gaia DR3 survey analysis.
